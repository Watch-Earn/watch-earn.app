Advertiser & Agency FAQ

Common questions about inventory, integration, and pricing.

How do you verify users are real humans in India?

We enforce multi-tiered verification: statutory 18+ declaration, Indian mobile number SMS OTP verification, and hardware-backed Google Play Integrity attestation that permanently blocks emulators and bots.

Which Mobile Measurement Partners (MMPs) do you support?

We support all major MMPs including AppsFlyer, Adjust, Branch, Singular, and Kochava, as well as direct Server-to-Server (S2S) postbacks authenticated via HMAC SHA-256 signatures.

What is the minimum test campaign budget?

Pilot campaigns start at ₹25,000. This enables sufficient sample size to establish conversion benchmarks and validate postback attribution.

Can we target specific geographic regions or demographics in India?

Yes. We offer targeting across Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 cities, state-level geo-fencing, age brackets, and device performance tiers.
