Frequently Asked Questions

What is the coin conversion rate?

The rate is fixed: 1,000 coins equals exactly ₹1.00 (100 paise). 10,000 coins equals ₹10.00 (1,000 paise).

What is the minimum withdrawal amount?

The minimum withdrawal is 10,000 coins (₹10.00). Withdrawals must be in multiples of 10 coins so paise are always integers.

How are payouts sent?

Payouts are transferred directly to your verified Indian UPI Virtual Payment Address (e.g., username@bank).

Why do coins go into "Pending"?

When an activity is reported by our partner, coins enter a brief verification hold period (typically 24 hours) to verify traffic validity and prevent fraud before becoming available for withdrawal.

Who is eligible to participate?

Indian citizens and residents aged 18 years or older with an active Indian mobile number and valid UPI ID.
