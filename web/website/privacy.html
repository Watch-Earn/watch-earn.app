Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 • Compliant with Indian DPDPA (2023)

1. Information We Collect

We practice strict data minimization:

  • Mobile Number: Stored cryptographically hashed (SHA-256) for authentication and fraud prevention. Only the last 4 digits are stored for display.
  • UPI VPA: Necessary only to execute approved withdrawal requests.
  • Device Signals: Device model, OS version, and Play Integrity tokens to prevent emulator abuse.

2. Purpose of Processing

Your data is processed strictly to: (i) maintain your account session, (ii) verify partner activity callbacks, and (iii) execute and reconcile UPI payouts.

3. Account & Data Deletion

You have the fundamental right to erasure under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act. To delete your account and personal identifiers, submit a request via the app profile settings or email privacy@watch-earn.app. All personal identification tokens and phone hashes will be purged within 30 days. Immutable financial ledger entries are preserved in pseudonymized form for statutory accounting audits.
